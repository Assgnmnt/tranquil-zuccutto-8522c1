Questions you may be carrying.

Why only five women?

The room requires intimacy to work. Five is the number that lets every voice be heard and the witness be real, not symbolic.

Do I need to know my assignment before I join?

No. You need enough of a sense of it to begin naming and building it with structure.

How long are the weekly sessions?

Approximately 90 minutes, live, once a week for four weeks.

What if I miss a session?

Reach out to Jackie directly, since the Lab is small and cumulative, missed sessions are handled case by case.

Will I leave with the same documents as the Intensive?

Yes, your Assignment Brief PDF and Activation Map, built over four weeks instead of one session.
