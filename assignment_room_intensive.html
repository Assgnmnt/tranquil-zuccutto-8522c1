What Happens In The Session

Five steps. One documented outcome.

Not an open-ended conversation. A structured sequence that moves you from a felt sense of calling to a specific, documented assignment before you leave.

1
Ladder Audit — name what you've been standing on, and why stepping off has felt risky.
2
Breach of Agreement — formally break with the belief that's been running the delay.
3
Mandate Statement — put language to what you're building, for whom, and why now.
4
Activation Map — turn the mandate into milestones and a first 14-day sprint.
5
Assignment Brief — everything above, compiled into one document before you leave.

You don't need a polished idea. You need to come prepared to tell the truth about where you are and what's been keeping you from moving.

You leave the session with your completed Assignment Brief. It's produced during the intensive, not as homework afterward.
