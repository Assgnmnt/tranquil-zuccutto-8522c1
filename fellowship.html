Questions you may be carrying.

Do I need to have completed every prior room first?

Not necessarily every room — but you need the foundational work done: your Assignment Brief, a sustained body of work, and clear evidence you've moved past definition and early activation.

What does the application process look like?

Submit a substantive application, get a careful review within a week, and if it indicates readiness, a discernment conversation before Fellowship is confirmed.

What is the investment?

Shared after your application is reviewed and a discernment conversation has taken place. Each pathway is specific to the woman and her form of multiplication.

What if I apply and I'm not ready?

You'll get an honest response, including which room in the pathway is the more appropriate next step. Applying early isn't a failure — it's information.
