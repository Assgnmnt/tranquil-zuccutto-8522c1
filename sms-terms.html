Program description

The Assignment Room™ offers a text message program that sends you updates about our programs, cohort and event reminders, and occasional messages and offers from Jackie Parker and The Assignment Room™. By opting in, you agree to receive these messages.

How to join

You join by entering your mobile number on an Assignment Room™ form and checking the box that agrees to receive text messages. We will not text you unless you opt in.

Consent is not a condition of purchase

You do not have to agree to receive texts to buy anything from us or to join any program.

Message frequency and costs

Message frequency varies. Most people can expect a few messages per month, with more around enrollment dates and live events.

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message at any time. You will get one confirmation text and no further messages after that. You can rejoin at any time by signing up again.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message, or email support@assignmentroom.com.
